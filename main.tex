                    \item<7-> \textit{Surface-unaware}: same sampling regardless of reflectance complexity
                    \item<8-> Lead to undersampled or oversampled datasets
                    \item<9-> Lack of objective dataset quality assesment methods
                    
                \end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Motivation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lack of computational frameworks for surface classification or anomaly detection
    \item RTI data encodes rich directional reflectance $\rightarrow$ used mostly for \textit{interactive relighting}
    \item Open archaeological challenges (fragment assembly, translucent artifacts) remain underexplored in computational RTI. 
        \begin{itemize}
            \item More focus on visualization and modeling in the field.
        \end{itemize}
